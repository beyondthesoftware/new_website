Momentum Lab Sprint

Crew Efficiency

A practical sprint for Operations leaders who want to understand what crew efficiency is really telling them, and what to do about it before the season ramps up.

Explore All Sprints Join This Sprint

Why This Sprint Matters

Crew efficiency is one of the most talked-about metrics, but do you really know what to do with it?

Most teams can see the numbers. Fewer trust them. Even fewer know how to translate them into decisions that actually improve performance.

This sprint will take a look back at last year's data with fresh eyes to separate signal from noise. You'll identify which efficiency metrics actually matter, where they come from, and how reliable they are so that you can make informed adjustments now before the busy seasons kicks in.

Who This Sprint Is For

This sprint is for those managers and directors who want to learn how to make data-driven decisions around field operations. It's a great fit if:

Your Role

  • You are a Director of Operations or Operations Manager, or you are an Owner, GM or Branch Manager heavily involved with operations
  • You are responsible for crew productivity, labor performance or job execution
  • Regularly field questions about labor efficiency without feeling confident in the answers

Your Current State

  • You can see crew efficiency numbers in Aspire, but aren't sure how much to trust them
  • Suspect last year's data holds insights you haven't fully unpacked
  • You want to make adjustments before the season picks up, rather than mid-crisis

Your Mindset

  • Consider yourself a beginner to intermediate Aspire user, or have at least one year of consistent data in Aspire
  • Are willing to look honestly at the data, even if it challenges your assumptions
  • Can commit at least ~2 hours/week to focus on analysis and action

What You'll Get

You'll walk away with:

  • A clear understanding of which crew efficiency metrics actually matter, and why
  • Visibility into where those numbers come from and what influences them
  • Confidence in the integrity (or limitations) of your data
  • A repeatable process for reviewing efficiency without guesswork
  • Practical ideas for addressing inefficiencies that are within your control

And most importantly: you'll stop treating crew efficiency as a mysterious scoreboard and start using it as a tool to make better operational decisions.

Week-by-Week Breakdown

This sprint is designed to slide into your already busy schedule. Each phase is designed to take about two hours, with progress building week over week so you're never trying to fix everything at once.

Week 1: Build the Foundation

Get grounded in what crew efficiency actually represents in your operation.

You'll identify the key efficiency metrics you want to evaluate, understand how they're calculated, and locate where the data lives in Aspire. This week focuses on establishing a clean starting point and aligning on what "good" even means in your context.

Week 2: Discover and Validate

Dig into last year's data and pressure-test what you're seeing.

You'll trace efficiency numbers back to their inputs, identify potential sources of distortion, and assess data integrity. This week is about asking better questions and learning how to spot patterns worth paying attention to.

Week 3: Analyze and Act

This is where we turn insights into decisions.

You'll analyze trends, compare crews or time periods, and identify where efficiency gaps may be driven by process, scope, scheduling, or data issues. A 1:1 support call helps you work through a real scenario and decide what actions are worth taking now versus monitoring later.

Week 4: Sustain and Apply

Lock in what you've learned and prepare for the season ahead.

You'll document your review process, decide how often to revisit efficiency metrics, and identify one or two concrete changes to test as the season ramps up. The goal is not perfection, it's clarity, consistency, and readiness.
